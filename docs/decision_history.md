# Five decisions that explain the project

| Question at that point | Evidence | Problem learned | Response | Result retained |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Can a fixed PRKDC genetic nomination guide drug priorities? | SPIDR and two DDRi settings; 12 of 36 source calls evaluable | Unequal coverage and unstable preferential ordering | Bound the conclusion to the observed panel | Historical no-escalation, not complete-rule invalidation |
| Does a particular repair/metabolism crossing recur? | Fixed modules, 16,549-gene R2 background | Both external contrasts are positive; the second required sign fails | Retain the fixed failure; do not choose another pair to rescue it | +0.324207 / +0.154191 |
| Can a mismatch-design series explain intervention differences? | Wilson design metadata | Full manufactured-variant/parent mapping unavailable | Close that resource-dependent route | BLOCKED, not a biological negative |
| Does a positive mitochondrial/comparator balance survive support changes? | v3 integer-count export, fixed pathways and six views | Comparator retention is highly asymmetric; targeting units also change | Disclose both components and all added/retained members | M approximately stable, E changes; balance reverses |
| Is more analysis required for this version? | Reconstructed core workflows and bounded conclusions | Broader biology remains unidentified by the same archive | Stop scientific expansion; improve clarity, attribution and reuse | Frozen portfolio case study, not target validation |

These are separate questions with a traceable history, not sequential validation of a single causal mechanism. Failed, blocked and rejected outcomes have different meanings. Freezing retains evidence; it never prevents correction of a demonstrated error in a documented new version.
