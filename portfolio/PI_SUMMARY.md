# Project summary

**DDR TargetBridge — comparator membership in a DNA-PK inhibitor screen**

This completed computational case study asks whether a pathway-prioritization argument survives changes in its analyzable population. It uses public A549 WT/PRDX1-KO × vehicle/AZD7648 screening records and separates additional targeting-unit support from additional genes.

The mitochondrial component is approximately stable, while expansion of the translation-elongation comparator reverses their relative contrast. Expanded support also reaches the count floor. The result identifies dependence on comparator composition; it does not establish a corrected biological direction, pathway mechanism or general prioritization algorithm.

The project contributes a source-linked implementation and finite empirical analysis with AI assistance. Earlier unsuccessful comparisons remain separate histories rather than validation of the main finding. Original investigators generated all experimental evidence. Personal ownership is documented separately and must reflect actual involvement.

The unresolved scientific question is how to distinguish treatment-specific dependence from growth, selection and measurement effects in a suitably controlled design. No additional model or new cohort is needed to complete this bounded case study.
